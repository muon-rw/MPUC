Gameplay:
- Fix "error"/"illegal" enchantments appearing in loot
- Many fixes from Ice and Fire
- Players can now jump into Supplementaries cannons. They also added a piñata
- Fix deep aether structures and biomes not being locatable with the `/locate` command or the Eye of Brass
- Many new copper items from vanilla backport
- Fix spellbinding tables appearing in some Integrated Villages
- Fix eyes from Mowzies Cataclysm and Cataclysm'Rise duplicating when thrown
- Goblins, Hobgoblins, and Githyanki now see tooltips for which gear works for their bonuses
- New armor set, sheathe, and 3 weapons from Discerning the Eldritch, and a new crafting material Starmetal found in end city treasure. 
- Fix aptitudes (Strength, Dexterity, etc.) resetting to 1 on dimension change
- Magic damage will now show its spell school color in damage numbers

Balance:
- Spell nerfs: Brimflame Bolt, Brimstone Hellblast, Holy Blast, Evercomet Barrage, Dazzling Obliteration, Spectral Hammer, Fortify
- Nerfed the output of create gem/affix crushing
- Lowered the spawn rates of void wanderers, wraiths, river golems, and wandering ribbits by 75%
- Lowered the loot rate of ancient tomes by ~90%
- Fix weapons with restricted enchantments dealing less damage than intended 
- Max armor durability lost per hit 20% -> 5%

Performance:
- Lowered resolution of the main menu panorama, which fixes menu lag on older GPUs
- Re-enabled JEI tooltip/tag search, which should no longer lag on every keystroke or lag on startup.
- Disable JEI enchantment/armor trims/grindstone recipes, which were crowded. This also saves ~100MB of RAM. (Use Apoth's enchanting table menu or test in an anvil for enchantments)
- Halved the performance impact of player rendering cost (Apoli/Origins)
- Reduced allocation rate from various mods by ~80%, meaning less lagspikes on low-RAM systems
- Fix a few mods' retained heap (again, low-RAM system improvements, mostly fewer spikes)
- A few more targeted render thread patches, improving overall FPS modestly.
(Mods tweaked for allocation/heap/cpu: FTB Chunks, Bathymetry, AAA particles, Iron's Spellbooks, Fancymenu, Create: GnK, Icy's Better Horses, JEI, Apoli, Biolith, Brewin n' Chewin, Lithostitched, VanillaBackport)
- Pre-run all old-structure DFU upgrades, saves ~300mb of RAM
- Evict old cached structure templates, helps RAM usage on very long game sessions
- Enabled Modernfix RL and Climate Param deduplication. Adds a few seconds of startup time, lowers RAM by ~50mb
