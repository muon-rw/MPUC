### Gameplay
- A new animated fantasy HUD. Integration with temperature, thirst, vampirism, bandages, potions, and food! 
- Artificers have received a **total rework**! 
- **New abilities**: Tool Mastery, Flash of Genius
- - **Armorer**: Guardian Mode / Infiltrator Mode, Defensive Field / Lightning Launcher, Perfected Armor
- - **Artillerist**: Eldritch Cannon (modes: Force Ballista, Flamethrower or Protector), Explosive Cannon, Arcane Firearm, Fortified Position
- - **Battle Smith**: Steel Defender (metal doggy), Arcane Jolt. Other stat boots
- - **Alchemist**: New preset elixirs added to choose from via a radial menu, and more buffs from chemical masteries
- Added a few new Draconic Ancestries, and each ancestry now maps onto one spell school. 
- - Ancestries now grant school access on top of the existing spell power bonus
- - Changed some Dragonborn elemental breath attacks and spell power bonuses to line up with these Sorcerer changes
- Removed enchantment restrictions. Totally gone! 
- Reduced the fire spread factor for logs and leaves, so forest fires will get out of control less often. 
- Added Legendary Survival Overhaul's purified water as a liquid for the purposes of Create and Sophisticated Backpacks
- Added Ice Spider Dens to the structure regen list
- Taiga biomes now require much more leaves nearby to play huge echoey forest floor ambience tracks
- All players can see repair materials on item tooltips now, not just artificers
- Lowered the spawn rate of Aquamirae anglerfish and maws
- Bandage healing per second increased from 0.1 -> 0.5
- Removed cataclysm's cursed pyramid from overlap prevention, to make it more common
- Nerfed the damage boost from Sharpshooter by about 50%. Velocity unchanged
- Sharpshooter progress now scales with draw speed. 

### Fixes
- Fix Might/Power enchantments not working when using custom arrows. 
- Fix Endless Quiver not working on certain bows, or for Archery spells
- Fix disabled enchantments still appearing in Apotheosis' enchanting table (this fixes enchanted books and items disappearing after you enchant them) 
- Fix campfires only healing 1 player at a time
- Fix Wildshape not working properly on dedicated servers
- Fix Butchery meat not being usable in Farmer's Delight recipes
- Fix Errored gem in the Nexus chest
- Fix Ch.6 questbook showing the wrong recipe for Obsidian Mirror
- Fix Ch.5 dragon rewards having items that didn't exist
- Fix duplicate music tracks playing in the nether
- Improved FPS when a wildshaped player was on screen
- Base stone from quark, regions unexplored, aether, deep aether, bwg, yung's cave biomes and betterend is now mineable with spectral hammer
